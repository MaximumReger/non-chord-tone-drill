# Non-Chord Tone Drill

A practice page for finding and naming non-chord tones.

Each example is one measure of 3/2 on a grand staff with a key signature. The bass staff has three block chords in half notes, labeled with Roman numerals and figures. The treble staff has a quarter-note melody with two or three non-chord tones: passing tones, neighbor tones, appoggiaturas, escape tones, suspensions, double-neighbor tones and anticipations.

Click a melody note. A non-chord tone turns green and a chord tone turns red. After you pick a non-chord tone, choose its type from the buttons. When you're right, the button turns green and a label appears above the note. Once every non-chord tone is labeled, **Generate another example** loads a new one.

Everything runs in the browser from `index.html`. Notation is drawn with [VexFlow](https://www.vexflow.com/).
